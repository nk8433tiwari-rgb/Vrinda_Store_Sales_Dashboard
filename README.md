Executive Summary
This project delivers an interactive Excel-based sales dashboard analyzing 28,471 unique orders and ₹21.17M in total revenue for Vrinda Store across 2022.
The objective was to transform raw transactional records into actionable business intelligence—evaluating key revenue drivers, customer purchasing patterns,
distribution channel performance, and regional sales performance to optimize retail growth and targeted marketing strategies.

Key Insights & Business Findings
Demographic Target: Female shoppers drove the majority of business, contributing 64% of total sales (₹13.56M), with adult women (ages 30–49) forming the primary customer base.
Dominant Channels: E-commerce marketplaces generated over 80% of total revenue, led by Amazon (35.5%), Myntra (23.3%), and Flipkart (21.6%).
Geographic Distribution: Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh emerged as the top 3 revenue-generating states, contributing nearly 35% of total sales.
Fulfillment Performance: Maintained a 92.25% delivery success rate, with combined cancellations and returns accounting for approximately 6% of orders.

Tools & Excel Techniques Used
Data Cleaning & Wrangling: Handled missing/null values, standardized date fields, and categorized continuous variables (e.g., Age Group binning into Teenager, Adult, and Senior).
Data Summarization: Leveraged advanced Pivot Tables and calculated fields to aggregate multi-dimensional retail metrics.
Data Visualization & Dashboarding: Designed a visual dashboard featuring dynamic charts, conditional formatting, and interactive multi-slicer filtering.

Strategic Recommendations
Targeted Marketing: Focus digital ad spend on Amazon and Myntra, targeting adult female consumers in high-performing regions (Maharashtra, Karnataka, and UP).
Inventory Optimization: Stock top-performing product categories (Kurtas and Sets) in high-demand sizes during peak purchasing months (March to May).
